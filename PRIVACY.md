# FilzaPDF Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 19, 2026

FilzaPDF is a desktop PDF reader and document utility.

### Information collection
FilzaPDF does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer. Files are processed locally on the user's device unless a feature explicitly communicates with an external service.

### Documents and files
Users choose which files to open, edit, merge, or convert. Users control where their documents are saved and shared.

### Third-party services
If future versions introduce analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or online services that collect information, this policy will be updated accordingly.

### Security
Users should keep their devices secure and maintain backups of important documents.

### Contact
For questions about this policy, visit the [FilzaPDF GitHub repository](https://github.com/Peshimam-Ashar-Mohammed/FilzaPDF).
